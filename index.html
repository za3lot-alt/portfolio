> ABOUT

I'm a 4th-year Software Engineering student at Halic University, Istanbul. My focus is at the intersection of full-stack web development and quantitative trading — building systems that can be backtested, monitored, and shipped end-to-end.

I spend most of my time learning the QuantConnect LEAN engine, writing Python strategies on forex pairs and gold, and walking them through honest out-of-sample validation rather than chasing in-sample curves. On the application side I work in PHP, JavaScript, MySQL, and increasingly Python — and I'm a committed vibe coder who treats AI tools (Claude, Copilot) as collaborators rather than oracles.

This portfolio is itself a Year-4 deliverable: a single-page application, a session-protected admin dashboard, and an honest live forex ticker — all built without a framework, on the principle that you should understand every line you ship.
